# su15 level 3: floor 11

Game su15-1944f8ab, level 3. Engine file su15.py, sha256 a5f91f7c963d6ca6447dae0ab21342b48a3f511601c40dfa8e972bdc59b4651e. The floor is 11 clicks, counted from the level start, with no RESET in front.

Class: matching bounds (lower bound = verified win). The lower bound comes from the optimum of an admissible relaxation, the lineage relaxation. That optimum is 11 at the start state and equals the replayed winning route of 11 clicks. The bound-10 exhaustion closes the last gap.

## Lower bound: no win in 10 clicks or fewer
1. The state is a multiset of pieces (value, x, y). Level 3 starts with six pieces of value 0 and three pieces of value 1; the mass is 12. The goal is one piece of value 3 and one of value 2 in the two zones.
2. A click moves only the pieces within distance 8 of it. Every hit piece lands at a place that depends only on (value, click) and contains the click point (exhaustive over 8,651,902 instances, 0 exceptions).
3. No click raises the mass and the goal needs all 12, so a winning route has no lossy click. A useful click moves one piece or merges two equal pieces, or it is the joint final click from {value 3, value 2}.
4. Undo, collisions, no-ops and the step budget never create a new state, so they cannot shorten a win.
5. A shortest win is a forest of lineages. Dropping all interaction between lineages gives a relaxed problem. Its value D[subset][position] (150 piece subsets x 4096 positions) is computed exactly by a layered search with move and merge edges taken from the simulator. The joint mid-flight final click is included.
6. The relaxed value at the start state is 11 (the minimum over the normal final click, the joint both-hit final click and the joint single-hit final click). Three independent codes give 11: `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p1/p1dp.h`, `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p2/dpb.c` and `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/check/src/rdp.c` (written from su15.py only). The cost histograms over all 614,400 cells equal each other.
7. Bound-10 exhaustion: the start state is expanded (4096 clicks, 992 distinct children). Every child has a relaxed value of at least 10, which is above the 9 clicks left, so every child is pruned and no win exists. `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p1/logs/nodeA/exh10_rerun_shard0.log`, `exh10_rerun_shard1.log`, `exh10_rerun_shard2.log`, `exh10_rerun_shard3.log` and `exh10_rerun_shard4.log` in the same folder (5 shards, rc 0) show NO_WIN; `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p2/logs_nodeA/ida_bound10.log` prunes at the root (11 > 10).
8. Controls: the same search at bound 11 finds 11-click wins, which replay on the engine. Control: with the mid-flight win test switched off, the search does not find the 11-action win.
9. The simulator equals the engine: 0 mismatches over 16,000 and 6,000 random walks, 605 and 419 states x 4096 clicks, 67,048 targeted clicks and 55,375 walk actions with undo.

Two proofs, `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p1/PROOF.md` and `proofs/certificates/su15_L3/p2/PROOF.md`, were written with independent code. ## Scope and limits
- The two proofs use the same method family, the lineage relaxation. Independence is in the code and the checks.
- No state-by-state search to depth 10 exists. The depth-10 claim rests on the relaxation, in which every step is machine-checked exhaustively per piece and per pair, plus whole-state checks.
- The simulator equals the engine by lockstep sampling plus exhaustive landing tables. This is not a formal equivalence.
- Only the offline engine was used.
